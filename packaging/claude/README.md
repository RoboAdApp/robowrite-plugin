# RoboWrite for Claude

Use RoboWrite to plan articles from keyword research and competitor insights, review briefs in your brand’s voice, and reopen saved Markdown drafts. When you attach source documents or enable knowledge-library retrieval, RoboWrite can bring relevant passages into the writing workflow. This gives Claude context such as your product terminology and editorial guidance when you work on SEO or generative engine optimization (GEO) content.

## Connect your workspace

1. Open **Plugins** in Claude, find **RoboWrite** in the Anthropic Directory, and select **Add**. Check the version and skills shown on the listing before connecting.
2. Connect RoboWrite and sign in or create your RoboWrite account in the browser. Review the permissions on the consent screen. If you connect from Claude Code, open its MCP menu to start sign-in.
3. Ask **“Which RoboWrite workspace am I using?”** If Claude offers eligible workspaces, choose one by name. Claude checks that account before opening your content. With multi-workspace access, you can ask to switch in chat. Existing single-workspace grants keep their original access.

## Try it with your own content

- **Get started.** “I’m new to RoboWrite. Help me choose my workspace and set up my first brand manually.”
- **Recover a draft.** “Find our most recent saved draft about customer onboarding. Show me its title, version and the opening paragraph.” RoboWrite can reopen saved work without starting another writing job.
- **Review research.** “Open the existing brief for our onboarding article. Which keywords, competitor pages and internal sources does it already include? Flag any gaps.”
- **Plan an article.** “Use our brand voice and the attached product guide to propose an outline for the keyword ‘customer onboarding checklist’. Show me the plan before saving anything.”

## Data and usage

Claude sends the arguments needed for the selected tool to RoboWrite’s hosted server. Depending on your request, these can include the chosen workspace, a brand or draft identifier, search terms, source URLs or text to save. RoboWrite returns the matching workspace records, saved content or job status to Claude. Writing or rewriting content uses your RoboWrite allowance and can incur charges if overages are enabled. Review the proposed action before starting a generation job.
